THE END / AN OPEN QUESTION

There is no single way
to carry a memory.

One person may keep an object close. Another may put it away. What might change if we asked what someone needs, instead of deciding for them?

This experience offers questions, not therapy or instructions about grief.

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE / 2026

Why this experience?

For: Adults who wish to better understand and support someone experiencing loss.

Goal: Explore how the same objects can mean different things to people, without giving a score or one correct response.

Use: A short personal web experience, discoverable through a project exhibition or a voluntary link. New stories are reviewed before publication. You can read without contributing.

Note: All characters and example notes are fictional. This is an educational design prototype, not a clinical tool or grief support service.
